## Immediate Valmont implications to investigate

These are kit-based interpretations, not measured counters or synergy ratings:

- His tether's Ground threatens Gideon's escape, but **Vein Splitter removes Ground**. Match advice must consider the selected augment rather than assigning every Valmont the same control.
- The tether can break when Valmont is immobilized or loses line of sight. Steel's control can therefore protect an ally as well as initiate a fight.
- Attack-speed theft is relevant to attack-dependent plans such as Serath's. Do not apply a blanket matchup penalty without considering whether the tether lands and remains connected.
- His ultimate's healing makes anti-heal application worth evaluating, while its growing mana drain changes sustained-fight planning. Muriel's existing anti-heal still needs an actual application; there is no automatic duplicate-item requirement.
- His sword's attack-speed inheritance does **not** establish that it applies every hero item on-hit effect. Validate pet/proc interactions before recommending an attack-speed item package for that reason.
