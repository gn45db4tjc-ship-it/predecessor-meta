# 1.17.2 strategy review: tiers, hero notes, counters, compositions and pools

Reviewed 2026-10-17 by Claude (editorial review), at the owner's request (decision card, 17 Oct). One-time review; it does not start recurring AI reviews. Gold+ is the editorial reference. Grades are dated editorial judgments, not calculated win probabilities. The 1.17.2 build pass earlier the same day (`docs/BUILD-PASS-1.17.2.md`) stays the plans' review; this pass reviews everything around them.

## Evidence

- **official**: Patch 1.17.2 (live 2026-10-13) and the 1.17 article with Hotfix 1.17.1, both fingerprinted in the packet. 1.16.4 is no longer live and no longer a fingerprinted source.
- **kits**: Pred.gg public kits and item definitions, version 169 (1.17.2), as collected for the Gold+ bundle published 2026-10-17 19:13 UTC.
- **pred_1172_gold**: Pred.gg ranked Gold+ 1.17.2 (ranks 27-36, version 169), collected 2026-10-17 13:19 CDT: about four days since the 13 Oct release, roughly 9,000 games per role and 78 to about 2,700 per hero and role. Role averages: carry 50.1%, jungle 50.6%, midlane 50.3%, offlane 50.0%, support 49.8%.
- **pred_1172_other**: the same cohort for Bronze+, Silver+, Platinum+, Diamond+ and Paragon. The ranks are nested (Bronze+ contains Gold+), so they are not independent; they appear only as each plan's six-rank reference.
- **reference**: each entry's previous sample: the 24 Sep 1.17 review sample or the 1.17 line sample (1.17 plus Hotfix 1.17.1) from the rechecks since.

## Method

- A grade moved at most one step, and only with a 1.17.2 kit or patch reason and evidence pointing the same way, or under the drop rule from the 27 Sep recheck: one step down without a kit reason when a 95% interval over at least 500 games excludes the previous reference rate. A high rate alone never raised a grade; samples under 100 games were not graded on alone.
- Every tier text, hero note, counter-pick, build adaptation, sequence note, damage profile, augment capability, composition and pool was re-checked against the exact 1.17.2 ability and item text. Text that cited "early 1.17" results as current, or stated a superseded number, was rewritten.
- Mechanics: every packet correction was re-checked against the 1.17.2 article and the Pred.gg 1.17.2 text. A pre-1.17 correction stays only where it is a clarification no 1.17 or 1.17.2 note contradicts (for example Tenacity rating units, Knell multi-hit, Vital Essence +5g).
- Each entry now cites its Gold+ 1.17.2 sample; the two roles under 100 games (Feng Mao offlane, 84; Serath offlane, 78) keep their previous sample and stay withheld until 100 current games exist. The earlier per-entry recheck dates and previous grades are dropped, because this review re-grades every entry; the recheck ledgers stay as history.

## Result

- **Tiers**: 86 entries; 16 grade changes; 70 retained, 42 of them with corrected text. On the Gold+ replay 84 tiers are active and none is flagged for moved statistics; 2 wait for 100 games.
- **Hero notes** 54, **counter-picks** 16, **build adaptations** 13, **sequence notes** 82, **damage profiles** 54, **compositions** 12: all active on all six ranks. Text changes: 5 strategy rows (Grux jungle and offlane Rapture adaptations, Kwang Q sequence note, Combat Veteran and Wild Growth capability reasons), 5 compositions, all 8 notes, 2 of 4 build-advice items, the pools.
- **Plans**: no plan field changed. Per-plan results: 89 checked and retained, 7 unresolved (the experimental roles, as in the build pass). 90 of 97 builds are active on every rank.
- **Mechanics**: 10 corrections retired because 1.17 or 1.17.2 replaced the value they set (Cannibalism, Patina, Bayle E text, Lt. Belica E cooldown and text, Revenant Q text and R cooldown, Terra E text, Combat Veteran, Scarlett Q text); 3 Pred.gg 1.16.4 text resolutions retired because Pred.gg 169 states them; the three reviewed definitions (Frost Snap, Harmonic Currents, Mending) retired because Pred.gg 169 supplies them. 113 plan preconditions and 87 strategy ability texts were re-pinned to the 1.17.2 text.

## Limitations

- About four days of 1.17.2 data; intervals are roughly 2 to 11 points either way. Matchup rows pool all roles.
- The `patch-1.17.json` supplement stays bound to 1.17: its corrections and hero context are inactive on 1.17.2, and Valmont's midlane plan reaches 1.17.2 through the build pass. The meta validator now accepts a supplement plan whose own patch matches the packet.
- Pred.gg's Murdock passive still shows the pre-1.17 36-60% text; the plan precondition keeps that text so the plan matches the source, and no displayed text quotes the number.
- On the saved Diamond+ and Paragon bundles the Pred.gg collection was partial and served a 16 Oct cached perk text (Vital Essence +4g; Sanguine Slash on Paragon), so the "Official correction review" notice names them there until the next full collection. The site shows the notice; it does not guess a replacement.
- No in-game test. Other ranks show the engine's calculated tiers; reviewed grades describe Gold+ only.
