# 2.32 build review — every hero/role plan against live 1.17

Reviewed September 23, 2026 by Claude (Opus 5.5) editorial review. One-time review: it does not start recurring AI reviews and it does not mark tiers, compositions or strategy current. Gold+ is the editorial reference; the six ranks were compared separately and never pooled.

## Evidence and access

- **official**: Patch 1.17 (live 2026-09-22) and 1.16.4 articles as collected 2026-09-23 14:24 CDT, fingerprints 271b9e0e… (1.17) and 0ca8b107… (1.16.4). No hotfix published; confirmed on predecessorgame.com 2026-09-23.
- **mechanics**: Pred.gg public definitions collected by the Windows app 2026-09-23 14:24 CDT (evidence/windows-bronze-2313.json), cross-checked against Omeda and the official notes.
- **pred_117**: Pred.gg 1.17 ranked Bronze+ role and item-position samples, 2026-09-23 14:24 CDT. The only current-patch outcome sample available; Pred.gg returned no structured data to later automated requests, which was respected.
- **pred_1164_gold**: Pred.gg 1.16.4 Gold+ samples, 2026-09-21 (previous patch).
- **statz**: Statz dataset labelled 1.16, collected 2026-09-23 14:52–14:56 CDT for all six ranks; broad dataset, previous patch.
- **limitation**: No Gold+ 1.17 outcome sample exists. Loadout (augment/Eternal) evidence is 1.16 only; 1.17 reduced Weald, Satariel and Lich, which is weighed where those variants lead.

## Result

- 97 verdicts: **45 changed**, **42 checked and retained**, **10 unresolved** (seven experimental roles plus three roles that are offered but have no authored plan).
- Every non-experimental plan is an active reviewed build on all six published ranks and on the Windows data: **87 of 87** on each state. The seven experimental plans show their dated 14 September plan with the stated limitation.
- Match adaptation ("Adapt to my match") is available for all 87 active builds. It is authorized by a separate adaptation review bound to the exact item-need and hero-kit classifications, which are identical on all seven data states.
- A changed item, ability or perk withholds exactly the builds that use it (checked by mutating Elafrost, Tainted Guard, Aurora Q, Krix and Satariel on both data states).
- Source reconciliation: 134 exact field transitions, none left unresolved. Phase Support and Valmont Mid are accepted with their disputed clauses stated, because neither plan depends on them.

- Tiers, compositions, counter picks, damage review and strategy were not reviewed and stay dated 14 September (1.16.4). Because the damage review is not current, enemy primary damage types are unknown, so armour needs in "Adapt to my match" apply only when you choose them.
- Level-by-level skill orders were not re-endorsed; the reviewed skill priority and the existing validated schedules are unchanged.
- Sniper's Bounty: the current source lowers non-hero killing-blow gold from 7 to 6 without an official note; recorded as a public-definition value. Demonic Bargain's source text renders the new schedule as 'Levels 1/0'; the official 1/10 schedule is relied on.

- Zinx Carry (Diamond+, 89 games), Rampage Offlane (Paragon, 10 games) and Legion Midlane (Bronze+ only) are offered by the data but have no authored plan.

## Engine and presentation changes

- Adaptation gate: an active patch-reviewed build can be adapted when a dated adaptation review for the same patch matches the current item-need and hero-kit classifications exactly. The overdue global strategy review no longer blocks it; a classification change withholds adaptation without touching the build.
- Tank-buster wording: the armour-shred/health-damage rule now accepts the current renderer's typed wording ("Max Health as physical damage") the same way as the published untyped wording. Before this, Windows data dropped Infernum, Megacosm, Mutilator and Sky Splitter from that rule.
- Evidence state reports build readiness and adaptation readiness separately from the tier/strategy review; the status strip shows "Builds · v1.17 · N/94 ready · Match adaptation reviewed" beside "Tier/team review pending".
- Patch-plan loading counts retained verdicts correctly (it assumed every patch-file plan was "changed").
- The guidance validator checks the adaptation review (patch, date, method, limitations, exact classification shape).

Probes written before each fix failed on the released engine; their outputs are kept with the review evidence.
