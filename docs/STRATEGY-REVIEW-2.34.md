# 2.34 strategy review: tiers, strategy, counters, compositions and three new plans for live 1.17

Reviewed 2026-09-24 by Claude (Opus 5.5) editorial review. One-time review at the owner's request; it does not start recurring AI reviews. Gold+ is the editorial reference. Grades are dated editorial judgments, not calculated win probabilities.

## Evidence

- **official**: Patch 1.17 (live 2026-09-22) and 1.16.4 articles; no 1.17 hotfix published at review.
- **pred_117_gold**: Pred.gg ranked Gold+ 1.17 (ranks 27-36), collected by the Windows app 2026-09-23 21:27-21:31 CDT: about 1.5 days of the patch, roughly 1,000 games per role and from fewer than 30 to about 770 per hero/role. Intervals are wide (about 4 to 18 points either way).
- **pred_117_bronze**: Pred.gg Bronze+ 1.17 (ranks 21-36), 21:19 CDT. It contains the Gold+ games, so it is nested, not independent.
- **review_1164**: the 14 Sep 1.16.4 Gold+ review samples (large, previous patch).
- **statz**: Statz still labels its dataset 1.16; used only for comparison.
- **plans**: the 23 Sep 2.32 build review (every plan reviewed against 1.17), read as the current plans.

## Method

- A grade moved only with a kit or patch reason and 1.17 evidence pointing the same way. A high rate alone was not a reason, and samples under 100 games were never graded on alone.
- Every tier text, hero note, counter-pick, build adaptation, composition and pool was re-checked against the exact 1.17 ability and item text. Text that stated 1.16.4 facts or named superseded plan choices was rewritten.
- Where a role has fewer than 100 current Gold+ games, the listed tier sample stays the 1.16.4 review sample and the app withholds the tier until 100 current games exist.
- Five role reviewers, a team reviewer with a cross-role consistency pass (9 fixes applied), a mechanics recertification and a new-plan author worked from the same frozen evidence.

## Result

- **Tiers**: 85 entries; 4 grade changes; the rest retained, most with corrected text. 70 of 85 tiers rest on the Gold+ 1.17 sample; 15 have fewer than 100 current games.
- **Hero notes**: 54; **counter-picks**: 16; **build adaptations**: 13; **compositions**: 12.
- **New plans**: Legion Midlane, Zinx Carry and Rampage Offlane (see below). Build review summary {'changed': 48, 'checked and retained': 41, 'unresolved': 7}.
- **On the Windows Gold+ 1.17 data** every reviewed item is active: 90 of 97 builds (the 7 are experimental roles), 70 tiers, 54 hero notes, 16 counter-picks, 13 adaptations and 12 compositions.
- **On the published six ranks** (no Pred.gg sample): 90 of 97 builds active; tiers wait for a current Pred.gg Gold+ sample; Khaimera and Mourn notes, and the 2 compositions with them, wait because the site still shows their older ability wording; 5 of 13 adaptations are active and 8 wait on item-text wording that differs from the reviewed 1.17 text.
