# 1.18 strategy review: tiers, hero notes, counters, compositions and pools

Reviewed 2026-11-08 by Claude (editorial review), on the owner's go of 8 Nov 2026. One-time review; it does not start recurring AI reviews. Gold+ is the editorial reference. Grades are dated editorial judgments, not calculated win probabilities. The 1.18 build pass of 7 Nov (`docs/rechecks/2026-11-07-2044.md`, PR #201) stays the plans' review; this pass reviews everything around them and adds Kaelen's first plan. It clears the recheck queue items `new-hero:kaelen` and `patch-change:19237cd8b9bf37`. Ledger: `docs/rechecks/2026-11-08-1700-ledger.json`.

## Evidence

- **official**: Patch 1.18 Frostbite (live 2026-11-03, `PatchNotes_1.18`) and Patch 1.17.2 (`Patch_Notes_1.17.2`), both fingerprinted in the packet and checked 8 Nov.
- **kits**: Pred.gg public kits and item definitions, version 170 (1.18), from the six live bundles published 8 Nov.
- **pred_118_gold**: Pred.gg ranked Gold+ 1.18 (ranks 43-53 after the 1.18 rank reset, version 170), collected 7 Nov about 21:51 CST: about four days since the 3 Nov release, roughly 2,300 to 2,400 hero games per role and 0 to 367 per hero and role. Role averages: carry 48.1%, jungle 48.4%, midlane 48.1%, offlane 48.8%, support 48.4%.
- **pred_118_other**: the same cohort for Bronze+, Silver+, Platinum+, Diamond+ and Paragon. The ranks are nested, so they are not independent; they appear only as each plan's six-rank reference.
- **reference**: each entry's 1.17.2 sample (the 17 Oct review or the rechecks since).

## Method

- A grade moved at most one step, and only with a 1.18 kit or patch reason and evidence pointing the same way, or under the drop rule (one step down without a kit reason when a 95% interval over at least 500 games excludes the reference rate). No Gold+ 1.18 sample reached 500 games, so the drop rule never applied. A high rate alone never raised a grade.
- A role under 100 Gold+ 1.18 games keeps its 1.17.2 sample and grade, and its watch line says so (36 of 86 entries).
- Every watch line was rewritten to cite its 1.18 sample (or why it keeps the 1.17.2 one) and to keep or correct the play advice against the 1.18 text. The displayed "why" lines, hero counterplay and counter-pick reasons were checked against the 1.18 ability text and needed no change; two counter-picks re-pin the 1.18 ability text they cite (Muriel's Alacrity and Reversal Of Fortune; Iggy's Flame Turret).
- A new hero's first grade rests on its kit and its first sample, and the review gate holds it for the owner.
- Mechanics: every packet correction was re-checked against the 1.18 and 1.17.2 articles and the Pred.gg 170 text.

## Result

- **Tiers**: 86 entries; 3 grade moves and Kaelen's first grade (B, held for the owner); 82 retained. Baron Valmont Midlane has no 1.18 grade (see Limitations).
- **Kaelen**: new jungle plan (Basilisk, Tainted Guard, Forticus; then Citadel, Aegis Of Agawar, Unbroken Will; Saphir's Mantle, Neverending Chase, Idrisil, Frost Snap and Glacier), counterplay, damage profile, two sequence rows (Winter's Pursuit, Frostfall) and a capability note for Neverending Chase. Its loadout definitions cite the six live checksum bundles.
- **Plans**: 93; 1 changed (Kaelen), 90 checked and retained, 2 unresolved (Akeron Support, Wukong Offlane). On the six-rank replay 90 of 93 builds are active everywhere except Diamond+ (89: Drongo Carry waits there because Diamond's retained Bring The Boom text predates 1.18).
- **Text**: all 8 notes, 4 composition weaknesses, the pools, title, summary and historical note rewritten for 1.18.
- **Mechanics**: 13 1.16.4 corrections retired because a later patch set the field again and Pred.gg 170 states the current value (Predator, Fracture, Shatterbloom; Argus, Bayle, Countess, Eden, Legion, Maco, Scarlett, Skylar ability rows); 6 rebased onto the Pred.gg 170 wording because the official value still holds but the tooltip moved. The 14 Pred.gg alternate preconditions now cite the 1.18 Gold+ pages; all 14 still match Pred.gg 170.
- **Official changes (UX hand-off F5)**: the parser gives the 1.18 article 56 hero rows over 16 heroes, 14 item rows, 30 perk rows and 19 unmapped rows. The packet maps 4 more by prose (Krix, Vesh and Aion Major Blessing rows; Iggy's Flame Turret), leaving 15 unmapped system or dash rows. The 3 Nov count of 157 rows over 37 heroes was an over-count.

## Limitations

- About four days of 1.18 data; no sample reached 500 games and intervals are roughly 5 to 15 points either way. Matchup rows pool all roles. The 1.18 rank reset moved Gold+ to ranks 43-53, so the 1.17.2 references come from a different rank numbering.
- Baron Valmont Midlane stays on its 1.17.2 review: his plan lives in the `patch-1.17.json` supplement, whose official definitions the validator accepts only at `Patch_Notes_<version>`, while the 1.18 article is at `PatchNotes_1.18`. The packet therefore has no 1.18 grade for him and the queue keeps `new-hero:valmont`. Changing the validator is the owner's call and is not part of this review.
- Platinum+ (Sanguine Slash) and Diamond+ (Bring The Boom, Vital Essence) still carry older retained Pred.gg descriptions, so the "Official correction review" notice stays visible there (held, as on 3 Nov) until a full Pred.gg collection on those ranks.
- Pred.gg 170 lists Tenacity as a percentage that disagrees with the official rating; it is not used for item choice.
- No in-game test. Other ranks show the engine's calculated tiers; reviewed grades describe Gold+ only.
